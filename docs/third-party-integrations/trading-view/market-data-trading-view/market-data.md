# Market Data Introduction

There are several flexible offerings for users to integrate data between Interactive Brokers and TradingView. Users are welcome to subscribe to any resource for their market data needs. Interactive Brokers does not require users to use IBKR-provided market data.

Interactive Brokers is required to warn customers about trading without live data. As such, if customers are trading without IBKR resources, we will warn users on each trade about the dangers, given we do not have a guarantee that the trader is making an informed decision.
