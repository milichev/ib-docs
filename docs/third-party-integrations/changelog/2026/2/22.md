# Third Parties

As of February 22nd, 2026, the connection from ESignal to Trader Workstation for delivering market data in TWS charts is terminated and no longer supported. This change is the result of a recent FINRA reporting requirement for fractional sizes that could not be supported with the connection. Please see [https://nasdaqtrader.com/TraderNews.aspx?id=UTP2025-18](https://nasdaqtrader.com/TraderNews.aspx?id=UTP2025-18)
