# Introduction

IBKR offers comprehensive reporting capabilities to meet your business requirements. This section provides detailed guidance on accessing various reports through our API.

* **[Activity Statements](activity-statements.md)**: Activity Statements reflect all account activity including cash transactions, dividends, corporate actions, and trades.
* **[Tax Forms](tax-forms.md)**: Access tax documents for the last 5 years.
* **[Trade Confirmations](trade-confirmations.md)**: Trade confirmation generated in real-time for all executions.
